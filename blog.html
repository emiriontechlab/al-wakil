Small Bathroom Upgrades That Still Feel Premium

Compact bathrooms need disciplined choices. Wall-hung sanitary ware, simple mixers, glass partitions, and lighter surfaces can make the room easier to use and visually larger.

Storage is important, but it should not dominate the layout. A slim vanity, recessed shelf, or mirrored cabinet often creates a cleaner result than multiple separate units.

With the right product selection, even a small guest bathroom can feel sharp, durable, and client-ready without unnecessary visual weight.
