About Al Wakil

Al Wakil Building Materials Trading LLC is a trusted supplier of sanitary ware and building materials in the UAE. Our showroom is located in Industrial Area 4, Sharjah. We serve homeowners, contractors, interior designers, and developers across Dubai, Sharjah, and Abu Dhabi. We are the authorized distributor of RAVENA Luxury Sanitary Ware in the UAE.
